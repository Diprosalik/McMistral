## **Features**

<br>

### **Real AI Chat**
**McMistral** integrates Mistral AI directly into your game. Ask about recipes, get survival tips, or just have a chat with your companion.

<br>

### **Full Game Context**
The AI knows exactly what you are doing! It reads your real-time data:
- **Coordinates & Facing Direction** (F3 debug data)
- **Health, Hunger & Status Effects**
- **Complete Inventory Contents**

<br>

### **Smart Commands**
If you explicitly ask the AI to perform an action (e.g., *"Make it day"* or *"Give me a workbench"*), it executes the matching Minecraft command automatically. Simple questions remain text-only to prevent cheating.

<br>

### **Mod Compatibility**
Thanks to Mixins, the AI automatically detects items and recipes from other mods installed in your modpack!

<br>

## **Setup**

1. Get an API key from the Mistral AI platform - https://admin.mistral.ai/organization/api-keys
2. In-game, type: `/mistral apikey <your_key>`
3. Chat with: `/mistral ask <message>`
